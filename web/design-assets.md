# Design assets

Original supplied logos are preserved under public/brand. Transparent native SVG lockups use the supplied five-band funnel silhouette; app/icon.svg contains only the funnel. Raster cutout attempts were rejected for edge artifacts and are not used.

Final hero: public/brand/career-hero.png, generated with the built-in image tool. Final prompt: “Refined editorial photograph of a young Black woman in a cream cotton shirt thoughtfully preparing a job application at a tidy oak desk in a calm contemporary home workspace. Candid three-quarter view, hands near laptop and CV papers, no readable text or invented logos. Warm window daylight, muted sage wall, leafy plant, believable skin and tactile detail. Magazine photography, warm white, oak and deep green palette. No cinematic effects, gradients, neon, floating UI, glowing shapes, illustrations or posed smile. Landscape 3:2 framing, person on right, attractive desk foreground.”

Motion uses a small solid-color progress rule and subtle hover movement; reduced-motion preferences disable it.
